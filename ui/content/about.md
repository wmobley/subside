---
title: About SUBSIDE
tagline: Helping Texans understand their risk of land subsidence.
---
**SUBSIDE** — the **Subsidence System for Insight and Data Exploration** — is a
statewide effort to bring Texas's scattered land-subsidence data into one open,
trustworthy place, and to help people understand what it means for them.

Led by the **Texas Water Development Board** with the **University of Texas at
Austin** and the **Texas Advanced Computing Center**, and supported by the
**U.S. Geological Survey**, SUBSIDE builds data-management, analytics, and
visualization services so subsidence data is *findable, accessible,
interoperable, and reusable* — supporting modern groundwater modeling and
water-resources planning across the state.
