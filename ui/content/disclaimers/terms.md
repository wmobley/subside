It is provided for informational and planning purposes only.

**No warranty.** The data, analyses, and tools are provided “as is,” without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including accuracy, completeness, merchantability, or fitness for a particular purpose. Subsidence measurements and predictions operate near the tolerance of the methods used; results may contain errors and may change as data, methods, and reference parameters improve.

**Not a regulatory or legal instrument.** Nothing in this system constitutes a regulatory determination, a certified survey, a permit decision, or legal advice. TWDB’s role with respect to this information is informational and non-regulatory. Results should not be used as the sole basis for design, construction, permitting, property, or legal decisions.

**Not a substitute for professional investigation.** Site-specific decisions require independent, professional engineering, geologic, or surveying investigation. Where quantitative use is intended, satellite-derived products should be anchored to ground-based measurements (GNSS and extensometers) as described in the project documentation.

**Provenance and currency.** Each product carries metadata describing its method, processing, quality-control level, and caveats. Review these before use. Data currency varies by collection; the system does not guarantee real-time or continuously updated results.
