---
title: Informational use only
---
The Risk Explorer shows observed ground movement derived from satellite radar (NASA OPERA) and related data for the area and time range you select. Results are prototype outputs, provided “as is,” and have not been validated for any specific site or decision. They are not a survey, not a regulatory determination, and not a substitute for site-specific engineering or geotechnical investigation. Always confirm against ground-based measurements before relying on a result.
